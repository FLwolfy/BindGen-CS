# Third-party notices

BGCS SDK and command-line products include third-party managed dependencies and native Clang resources. Their exact restored licenses and hashes are delivered in `licenses/dependencies` and `licenses/clang-resource`. Supplemental parser packages retain their independently verified native provenance and license inventory. The generated SPDX document describes actual delivered dependencies and embedded resources, independently of this human-readable index.

The optional English flat-identifier naming vocabulary in `BGCS.CSharp` comes from [`dwyl/english-words`, revision `7995690ebca6e7d5e1d56ad6cb1b446a358b2b79`](https://github.com/dwyl/english-words/tree/7995690ebca6e7d5e1d56ad6cb1b446a358b2b79). Its `words_alpha.txt` is distributed under the repository's [Unlicense dedication](https://github.com/dwyl/english-words/blob/7995690ebca6e7d5e1d56ad6cb1b446a358b2b79/LICENSE.md). BGCS changes only CRLF line endings to LF, preserving every one of the 370,105 entries, including the historical spelling in that pinned file. The source digest `fe1f2a9bf0d872f1c8367fc349e4a6f8e8300b06b6294ea5167483b2bc496127` becomes embedded digest `480a91457cb2e2e11226f0f37d91e8bfce2d9c02825e02a0b8d2de2a70a45bcc` after that single transformation. Exact provenance and original license text are delivered alongside SDK/tool dependency notices. Runtime-only and Contracts-only products do not embed this vocabulary.
